## About Me

I'm a fresh graduate in **Informatics Engineering (D3, GPA 3.79/4.00)** based in Bandung, Indonesia, working at the intersection of **software development** and **software quality**.

- **Fullstack Developer** — I ship end-to-end features across Laravel, React.js, FastAPI, and Flutter, from database schema to production UI.
- **Software Quality Assurance** — I pressure-test products with structured **black-box** and **vendor integration testing**, turning ambiguous requirements into clear, reproducible test cases.
- **Enterprise experience** — Built an internal system with **Laravel + React.js + PostgreSQL** and integrated **CAS SSO** at Museum Geologi.
- **Quality at scale** — Managed **102 bugs with an 84% resolution rate** during my SQA internship at PT Primalogic Global Teknologi.
